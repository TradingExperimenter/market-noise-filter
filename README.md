## Market Noise Filter

Most trading tools attempt to predict outcomes.

This tool highlights conditions where prediction
is least reliable.

It is designed to reduce false confidence,
not increase activity.

### Why this exists
Highly optimised markets tend to remove redundancy.
When volatility expands under low liquidity,
failure becomes nonlinear.

This tool makes that visible.

### Limitations
- Not a trading strategy
- Not suitable for short-term optimisation
- Will often tell you to do nothing

That is intentional.
